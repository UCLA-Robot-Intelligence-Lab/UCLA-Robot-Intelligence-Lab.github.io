Welcome to the UCLA Robot Intelligence Lab (URIL)!

Our goal is to build robots that assist people in everyday environments. Today's robots fall short: they need far more data than we can give them, act on models they cannot fully trust, and too often overlook the people they serve. Our research tackles these challenges through three pillars: data-centric skill learning, reliable foundation models for robotics, and human-centered autonomy.

Data-Centric Robot Skill Learning Robot learning is bottlenecked by scarce, uneven demonstration data. We build accessible collection interfaces, data-quality metrics, and simulated experience to make every demonstration count. Reliable Foundation Models for Robotics Foundation models produce fluent plans that robots cannot always trust or execute. We ground language and vision-language models in perception and action, adding structure and formal guarantees. Human-Centered Robot Autonomy Autonomy that ignores its users can frustrate them and erode their skills. We design robots and AI systems that infer intent, share control, and make the people around them more capable.
